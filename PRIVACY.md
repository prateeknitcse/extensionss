# Privacy

Scroll Leaderboard counts how far you scroll and shows it on a shared leaderboard.

**What the extension reads.** It watches scroll events on the pages you visit, only to measure distance in pixels. It does not read page content, URLs, form fields, or browsing history.

**What is sent to the server.** Your chosen username, a random secret that proves you own the username, a random device ID, and your total scroll distance for each day. Nothing else. No page addresses or site names are sent.

**What stays on your device.** The last 14 days of daily totals, your settings (unit, theme, paused, and the list of sites you chose not to track), and the secret and device ID. The site list never leaves your browser. The current tab's address is read only when you open the popup, to offer "Don't track this site".

**What other users see.** Your username and your distance totals (today, this week, all time, streak, best day).

**Controls.** You can pause tracking, stop tracking chosen sites, and delete your account from the popup (Settings, Delete my account). Deleting removes your username and all of your scroll history from the server. Accounts with no activity for 90 days are removed automatically, and usernames that were never used are freed after 7 days.

**Recovery code.** The secret is also your recovery code. Anyone who has it can act as your username, so keep it private.

**Third parties.** The server runs on a hosting provider and database provider chosen by whoever deploys it. The extension itself uses no analytics or advertising.
